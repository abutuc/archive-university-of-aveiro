Plants for Fun

Plants for fun is an organization that invests time into recovering injured plants. We have established an efficient team to portray not only the importance of plants in our ecossystem but also to reveal the ignorance of the common folk regarding this subject. According to the National Plan for Plant Recovery, more than 50% of the population forgets to water their plants leading to the chaos and mental breakdown of silly plant people. That's simply not right. We must come together to face the injustice of systemic plant bias. We must protect cactusus, we must protect arborial correspondency that can include nervous malfunction. We cannot silence our voices, we must shout and yell until our lungs come out of our screamy terried mouths. We shall reunite with the greater folk, we shall reunite with the chosen one. Thou shall pass what thou must face and thou shall stick what thou shall think. We must protect ourselves, we must protect the marginals that attack plants because only then true equality exists. We shall fight against the government of the world and pass a bill that will protect every single one in existence.
